## 科学解读与假设

### 结果解读

EMP v9.0.4 was used to analyze 132 samples and 470 level-7 taxa. Alpha diversity did not differ significantly by Group (Shannon Kruskal-Wallis p=0.290), Disease (p=0.170), or Phase (p=0.210). Bray-Curtis PERMANOVA showed weak overall four-group structure (R2=0.033, p=0.095), a small IBS-versus-UC compositional difference (R2=0.022, F=2.83, p=0.018), and no phase effect (p=0.627). PERMDISP for disease was p=0.363. Exploratory differential-taxon results showed lower Akkermansia muciniphila, Ruminococcus bromii, [Ruminococcus] torques, and Gemmiger formicilis, and higher Lactobacillus mucosae in UC (BH FDR 0.080-0.086); no taxon passed FDR<0.05.

### 可验证假设

Compared with IBS, UC is associated with a small, taxon-specific microbiome shift rather than a general loss of alpha diversity. UC communities are depleted in mucin- and butyrate-associated taxa, including Akkermansia muciniphila, Ruminococcus bromii, [Ruminococcus] torques, and Gemmiger formicilis, while Lactobacillus mucosae is relatively enriched. Proposed mechanism: UC-associated mucosal inflammation alters mucin turnover and fermentable substrate availability, selectively depleting these taxa and allowing acid-tolerant lactobacilli to expand. Testable prediction: an independent cohort should reproduce these directions and correlate with inflammatory markers after adjustment for medication, stool transit, and batch.

### 局限性

The data are educational, compositional, and small-effect. No taxon passed BH FDR<0.05, two samples lacked Group labels and were excluded from group inference, and before/after results were only trend-level after correction. Relative abundance cannot establish absolute expansion or depletion. No causal, diagnostic, or therapeutic claim is supported.
