# Week 5 DESeq2 Interpretation

I analysed 12 RNA-seq samples from treated and control groups. Each of batches A, B, and C contained two samples per condition, so I fitted `~ batch + condition` with control as the reference. The count filter kept 989 genes. In the PCA, PC1 explained 24% of the variance and placed all treated samples on one side and all controls on the other; PC2 explained 9%. With `padj < 0.05` and an absolute shrunken log2 fold change of at least 1, 60 genes were significant: 36 higher and 24 lower in treated samples. The pattern points to a broad response to treatment, although the small synthetic dataset and generic gene IDs mean I cannot assign a pathway or confirm a mechanism. I used AI to help run and debug the code, then checked the sample order, reference level, coefficient name, filtering rule, and final counts against the local output.
