## 科学解读与假设

### 结果解读

I analyzed 132 samples and 470 level-7 taxa in EMP Web v9.0.4. Group labels existed for 130 samples. Shannon diversity was similar across the four groups (Kruskal-Wallis p=0.290), between IBS and UC (p=0.170), and before versus after (p=0.210). The four-group community test was weak (Bray-Curtis PERMANOVA R2=0.033, p=0.095). IBS and UC differed slightly in composition (R2=0.022, F=2.83, p=0.018), with no dispersion problem (PERMDISP p=0.363). The before/after community test was not significant (p=0.627). UC had lower Akkermansia muciniphila, Ruminococcus bromii, [Ruminococcus] torques, and Gemmiger formicilis, and higher Lactobacillus mucosae. These taxa had BH FDR values from 0.080 to 0.086, so none passed FDR<0.05.

### 可验证假设

I hypothesize that UC affects a small number of gut taxa while overall Shannon diversity stays similar. UC samples had lower relative abundance of Akkermansia muciniphila, Ruminococcus bromii, [Ruminococcus] torques, and Gemmiger formicilis, and higher Lactobacillus mucosae. A possible mechanism is that UC-related mucosal inflammation changes mucin turnover and the substrates available for fermentation. Tests in an independent cohort should repeat the same directions and relate the taxa to inflammatory markers after accounting for antibiotics, medication, stool transit, and batch.

### 局限性

The data are educational and compositional, and the disease effect is small. No taxon passed BH FDR<0.05. Two samples lacked Group labels and were left out of group comparisons. The paired results were only trend-level after correction, and relative abundance alone cannot show absolute increases or decreases. The data do not support a causal, diagnostic, or treatment claim.
